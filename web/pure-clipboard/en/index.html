Pure Clipboard.

Clipboard history on your Mac. Off until you turn it on.

Download coming soonIn development · v0.8.0

The signed and notarized release for Apple silicon is not ready yet. The download button will appear when it is.

Pure Clipboard remembers what you copied and puts it back with one click. It is a native macOS app with no account, no cloud and no analytics.

What it does

Clipboard history

Text, rich text, images, PDFs, and files or folders from Finder, including multiple selections. For files only a link to the original is stored, never a copy.

Search and restore

Find an item by word, app or file name and put it back on the clipboard with one click. If an original is gone, the app says so plainly.

Under your control

Default limits: 100 items and 30 days. Set the size of an item and of the whole store, pause the history, delete one item or everything.

Skips what is sensitive

Content marked private or transient by a password manager is never stored. Text is checked for passwords, keys and tokens before it is saved.

AI and terminal on request

Optionally copies the final answer from Claude Code and Codex CLI, or a command's output through pclip. Termux on your phone transfers text only when you run a command.

Pure Remote

An overview of Tailscale connections between this Mac and your devices. It reads local state only and stores nothing.

Transfers to the phone

Send files from the Mac to the phone's Downloads folder and back, in chunks and resumable. SHA-256 check, nothing overwritten.

Privacy

  • The history is off by default. The clipboard is watched only after you explicitly turn it on, and only while the app is running.
  • Everything stays on this Mac in files readable only by you. Content and diagnostics are never sent to the cloud, and the app has no network code.
  • Images and PDFs are stored only after a separate consent. Before saving, the app scans them with on-device text recognition (OCR). OCR cannot find everything, so we do not promise that no secret can pass through an image.
  • Sensitive-data protection is a helper, not a guarantee. When in doubt, an item is not stored.

System requirements

  • A Mac with an Apple chip (Apple silicon).
  • macOS 14 or later. Tested on macOS 27; older versions are not verified yet.
  • Intel Macs are not supported yet.

Installation

Once the signed release is available, installation will be simple:

  1. Download the .dmg and verify its SHA-256 (the value will be shown next to the link).
  2. Open the image and drag Pure Clipboard to the Applications folder.
  3. Launch the app from Applications. No feature turns itself on.

Today only a test build signed ad hoc exists; it is not an official download.

FAQ

Does Pure Clipboard watch my clipboard?

Only when you turn the history on, and only while the app is running. By default the history is off and the clipboard is not watched.

Where is the data stored?

In ~/Library/Application Support/PureClipboard on your Mac. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Are passwords from a password manager stored?

No. Content marked private or transient is skipped. Text is also checked for passwords and tokens, but reliable detection cannot be guaranteed.

Are copies of my files stored?

No. From Finder only a link to the original is stored. If the original disappears, the app shows it.

Are images and PDFs safe?

They are stored only after a separate consent and an on-device OCR check. OCR does not recognise handwriting, QR codes or secrets that are not text, so enable this deliberately.

Does it work on Intel Macs, Windows or Android?

No. Apple silicon with macOS 14 or later is supported. Other platforms are not verified yet.

Is the app notarized?

Not yet. The signed and notarized release is waiting for a paid Apple Developer account. Until then it is not offered for download.
